
So far, we have a way to review a book. There is an AI editor that will review your book and give you opinions about it.

And then we can take action on that at the top level, where you tell the AI agent to update all the pages based on the review.

And also you can go to an individual page and modify that, and even take the note from the editor review and use it here directly.


We also have a way of generating a scaffolding for the kind of a list of objectives for each page and chapter, and then go through that and create pros for each of those pages based on that objective. And for this to happen effectively, we have a way to pass on text of the Bible of the book: the current page, previous page, and a summary of all the things which happened so far.
